The Offer

Visual Direction & Production

A commercial photography and film production built around one question: what does this property need to be understood as, before anyone arrives?

Who It's For

Independent properties that can't afford to be misread.

Built for independent hotels, boutique properties, villas and hospitality brands that compete on character rather than scale, and whose current imagery no longer carries that character accurately.

What Triggers a Project

Usually one of five moments.

Opening

A property preparing to sell rooms for the first time, with no visual language yet.

Renovation

Spaces have changed enough that the existing photography actively misleads.

Repositioning

The property is moving toward a different guest, rate or market than the one it was shot for.

Outdated imagery

The photography is old enough to work against bookings rather than for them.

An experience with no image

Something the property does well, a meal, a ritual, a view, has never been properly represented.

Site visit / planning still — 4:5
Before the Camera

What gets decided with the property first

Before any shooting happens, EXIF works through the same questions with the property:

  • Who the imagery needs to convince, and of what
  • Which spaces and moments actually carry the property's character
  • Where current representation and lived experience diverge
  • What the images need to do commercially: a website, a listing, a press kit, paid media
Process

How it runs, start to finish

01

Discovery call

A conversation about the property, the guest it serves and what triggered the project.

02

Direction

EXIF defines the shot list, sequence and intent, tied to the decisions made in the discovery call.

03

Production

On-site photography and film, directed in real time as light, staff and guests allow.

04

Edit & sequence

Selection, retouching and sequencing so the final set reads as one coherent representation of the property.

05

Delivery

Final files handed over with licensing terms and guidance on where each image performs best.

What's Produced

Photography and film, sequenced as one set

A body of still photography, and film where scoped, covering architecture, interiors, atmosphere, food and human moments, edited and sequenced to read as a single, coherent representation of the property rather than a stock library of individual images.

What the Client Receives

Final files, ready to use

Retouched, delivered images and (where scoped) film in the formats needed for the website, OTA listings, press and paid media, along with a short note on sequencing and where each image is intended to be used.

How Costs Are Structured

Four components, scoped per project.

Every Visual Direction & Production project is quoted individually once the discovery call defines its scope. The structure behind that quote stays consistent:

  • Creative direction and production Planning, shoot days, direction
  • Image licensing Usage rights, scoped to intended platforms
  • Travel and accommodation When the property is outside Katia's base
  • Equipment and production expenses Gear, assistants, incidentals
