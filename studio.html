INDEPENDENT VISUAL DIRECTION FOR HOSPITALITY.

EXIF works with hotels, villas and independent properties to understand what makes a place distinct, decide what deserves to become recognizable, and build the visual language around it.

BASED IN MEXICO /
WORKING WHERE THE PLACE TAKES US.

dear hotelier,

i deeply value the work you do.

i spent a few years inside hospitality observing from the outside (basically literally). i got to see up close all the work that goes into hitting those numbers, & how hard the people behind them work to make it happen.

somewhere along the way, i fell in love with observing.

then, i learned how to do it in a way that can actually help.

i worked across mexico & the caribbean, & started noticing something across different markets: there are so many things a property barely makes visible that their guests deeply appreciate.

that’s how exif began.

i wanted to build something around showing those things, understanding which ones matter & finding a way to make them visible.

i want to keep doing this because there are still so many places that deserve to be seen for what they really are.

see you soon,

katia

founder, exif
