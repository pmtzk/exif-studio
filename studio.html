EXIF / STUDIO

INDEPENDENT VISUAL DIRECTION FOR HOSPITALITY.

EXIF works with hotels, villas and independent properties to understand what makes a place distinct, decide what deserves to become recognizable, and build the visual language around it.

Based in Mexico. Working wherever the property is.

dear hotelier,

i deeply value the work you do.

i spent years inside hospitality observing with an external eye (almost literally). i got to watch quite closely all the work that goes into hitting those numbers, & how hard the people behind them work to make it happen.

somewhere along the way, i fell in love with observing.

since then, i’ve learned how to do it in a way that can actually help.

working across mexico & the caribbean, i kept noticing something: there are so many things a property barely notices that their guests deeply appreciate.

that’s how exif began.

i wanted to build something around noticing those things, understanding which ones matter & finding a way to make them visible.

& i made it my mission to make this work, because i genuinely believe in your vision & mine.

see you there,

katia

founder, exif
