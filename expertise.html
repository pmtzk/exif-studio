What we do.

Research, direction and creative work for the way a place is understood before arrival.

These are areas of responsibility. Your property’s priorities determine where we begin and what the engagement includes.

Three responsibilities. One considered direction.

Research & Representation AssessmentPositioning & Creative DirectionCreative Production & Implementation
The place.The promise.The perception.

01

Research & Representation Assessment

Investigate the property, its guests and commercial context. Examine what the experience offers, what guests notice, and what the current representation makes visible.

For an owner deciding what needs attention, an assessment can establish priorities before creative production is scoped. Its scope and price are proposed for the property.

Possible outputs
  • Research findings and representation review
  • Competitive and guest-perception observations
  • Priorities and recommendations

A setting.A feeling.A reason.

Choose what leads.

02

Positioning & Creative Direction

Define what the property should be known for and how that value is expressed. Connect positioning, narrative and visual decisions with the experience guests can actually expect.

For a new opening, repositioning, or a property reconsidering its presentation, direction gives the team and its collaborators a shared basis for decisions.

Possible outputs
  • Positioning direction and narrative systems
  • Creative briefs and visual identity direction
  • Copy and guest-facing communication

A decision, made visible.

03

Creative Production & Implementation

Translate approved decisions into materials and coordinate their use across the relevant channels. Photography, brand materials, websites and content can each be part of an agreed scope.

EXIF directs the work and coordinates specialized collaborators where needed. Implementation and specialist production are quoted according to scope, responsibilities and timing.

Possible outputs
  • Photography and coordinated production
  • Brand materials, website direction and digital content
  • Implementation plans and creative review

Outputs are examples. The proposal defines the deliverables, responsibilities and fee for your project. Different properties may begin with different work.

A starting point that fits the property.

A discovery conversation helps us decide whether investigation, a directly scoped project, or another route is appropriate.
