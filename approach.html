How a decision takes shape.

Observe, decide, create. Behind those three gestures is a considered process: understand, examine, define, translate and evaluate.

01Understand02Examine03Define04Translate05Evaluate
  1. 01

    Understand

    Context

    Start with the place and the people responsible for it. We discuss the guest experience, the property’s objectives, constraints and the decision that needs to be made.

    You contribute context, access and existing materials. We agree what needs investigation and what falls within the engagement.

    Inside this stage
    Your contribution
    You bring context, access and existing materials.
    What takes shape
    A shared question and an agreed scope.
  2. 02

    Examine

    Evidence

    Read the property’s current representation alongside guest feedback, booking and digital channels, competitive alternatives and available creative assets. Where useful, observation on site adds context.

    Evidence informs the work. We distinguish what the property knows, what guests report and what remains a question.

    Inside this stage
    Your contribution
    Current communication, available guest feedback and relevant alternatives.
    What takes shape
    Observations separated from assumptions.
  3. 03

    Define

    Priorities

    Identify what matters and establish priorities. Develop positioning, narrative and creative direction appropriate to the property and its commercial objectives.

    Findings, recommendations or a creative brief give the owner a basis for choosing the next step. Decisions are reviewed together.

    Inside this stage
    Your contribution
    The property’s objectives and a conversation about priorities.
    What takes shape
    A direction that can guide creative decisions.
  4. 04

    Translate

    Work

    Turn approved direction into creative materials: photography, copy, identity, web direction, digital content or guest-facing communication, according to the agreed scope.

    Production, specialist collaborators, approvals and timing are scoped explicitly. EXIF coordinates the creative responsibilities it has agreed to lead.

    Inside this stage
    Your contribution
    Approved direction, access and timely decisions.
    What takes shape
    Creative materials within the agreed responsibilities.
  5. 05

    Evaluate

    Review

    Review the work against the brief, the guest experience and the agreed priorities. Look at how it is implemented and what can be learned once it is in use.

    We define practical review criteria with the client. Where suitable data is available, it can inform adjustments and future work.

    Inside this stage
    Your contribution
    Feedback against the brief and the guest experience.
    What takes shape
    A considered review and practical next decisions.

Shared context. Clear responsibilities.

The depth of investigation and the creative outputs depend on the project. We agree scope, access, collaborators and review points before the work begins.
