EXIF / 2026

A place, made unmistakable.

Before a guest experiences a place, they experience its representation.

EXIF works with independent hospitality properties to define what should become recognizable before arrival—and produces the photography and film through which it is seen.

Before arrival

A place is never seen all at once.

It arrives in fragments: a room, a horizon, a certain hour, a detail someone decided to preserve.

Together, they become the place before the place is known.
