| Specific observed technique | Why it works | Adaptation principle | Why not repeat it literally |

| Original loader hands a photographic field to a split-color signature. | A change in composition marks arrival; the photograph and typography carry identity. | Keep that arrival; use precise spatial alignment and editorial cuts on new pages. | Replaying a cinematic loader in every chapter would consume attention and time. |
| Observe / Decide / Create deck assembles with scroll, then gives direct pointer/keyboard control. | Motion first explains a relationship, then the visitor chooses a reading. | Let service folios offer direct, reversible selection, with differentiated typographic treatment. | Three duplicate card decks would turn a distinctive interaction into a template. |
| Continuous photographic gallery allows arrows, wheel, drag, pause and native vertical scrolling. | The image sequence creates atmosphere while visitors retain control. | Treat the existing gallery as the photographic centerpiece; frame its entrance with spatial cues. | Do not add another scrolling engine or new photographs with unresolved rights. |
| Oversized cream navigation sheet reveals the blurred underlying scene. | Navigation belongs to the same visual world as the page. | Keep route titles stable and oversized; place hover interpretation in a separate margin. | Replacing a route name with small hover prose obscures hierarchy. |
| Dear EXIF expands from a property introduction into correspondence. | An ordinary task becomes a personal invitation through language, spacing and agency. | Give discovery qualification the rhythm of a letter, preserving all fields and truthful receipt. | Do not force the original letter's staged validation into the discovery form or imply a booking. |

## Creative direction selected from the available evidence

Three principal additions: an interactive service folio, a process reading spine with useful working-note disclosure, and a small original hospitality-language study. Correspondence and navigation receive compositional refinements. Normal page scrolling remains in charge. New visual material is authored typography and rules using existing identity tokens, not borrowed artwork or additional archive photographs.

In Practice has insufficient verified property evidence and publication-rights records for a case study. It becomes an explicitly labeled editorial exercise in observation/interpretation/decision; no client, empirical outcome or photographic transformation is claimed. Its modest scope is deliberate.
