02 / TRACES OF USE

Keep the signs
of living.

The slippers and linen are small details. Leaving them in the frame gives an amenity photograph a more inhabited reading.

Observe

What is visible.

Two chairs, a table, a hammock, slippers and an unmade foreground. These are observations from the photograph.

Interpret

What it suggests.

A place to pause, rather than an inventory of furnishings. An editorial interpretation, not a verified guest insight.

Decide

What stays.

Retain the traces of use. Give them enough space to be noticed. Do not polish away the reason this image feels lived in.
