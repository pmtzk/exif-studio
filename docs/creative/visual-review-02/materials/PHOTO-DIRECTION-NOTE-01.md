# Make use visible — draft photographic direction note

**Authored studio exercise for visual review. Not a client case or research finding.**

Source: `exif-gallery-03-terrace-hammock.webp`, existing photography from previous professional experience. The source pixels are unchanged.

**Visible evidence:** two chairs, a table, a hammock, slippers, linen and a terrace. Their presence is observable; their relation to guest preferences has not been verified.

**Editorial question:** are we showing what a room contains, or inviting a reader to imagine using it?

**Direction:** preserve the traces of use. Give the chairs, slippers and foreground enough space to remain legible. Keep the existing natural warmth. Avoid an inventory of furnishings.

**Illustrative communication:** “A place to pause.” One photograph remains larger than its caption. The proposed layout in the working-table board is the draft output of this note.
