PHOTO DIRECTION NOTE / 01STUDIO EXERCISE

Make use visible.

Visible material
A terrace, two chairs, a hammock, slippers and linen.

The most useful detail is already in the photograph.

The question

Are we showing what the room has, or what it invites someone to do?

The direction

Lead with the pause. Preserve the slippers and linen as traces of use. Keep the natural warmth; avoid turning the frame into an amenity checklist.

The image brief

Leave room around the chairs. Keep the foreground. Let one short caption support the photograph rather than describe every object.

Editorial hypothesis drawn from the image. Property-specific research would establish whether this interpretation is appropriate.

WHAT THE DIRECTION BECOMES

A place to pause.

A moment of hospitality, held in one frame.

An image brief becomes a photographic hierarchy, a short caption and a page composition. The subject stays larger than the explanation.

From attention
to something usable.

Research establishes what can be supported. Direction chooses what should lead. Implementation carries that choice into an image brief, words and a guest-facing composition.

Authored studio exercise, not a commissioned property case or a claim of research results. Existing photography from previous professional experience. The brief and layout are draft editorial materials created for this review.
