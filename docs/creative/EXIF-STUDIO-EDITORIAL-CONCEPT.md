# Studio concept: The way a place is seen

**Concept only; awaiting approval. Studio has not been implemented or edited.**

EXIF begins with a way of looking: at the experience a place actually offers, at what a guest notices, and at what its representation leaves out. Photography, hospitality sales and operations inform that perspective. They become connected lenses rather than a résumé. The page moves from seeing, to understanding, to making something visible, then hands over to the founder’s original letter.

## Composition and narrative

1. A quiet, confident opening: oversized cream/green serif title, a narrow introductory column, generous space. No credentials block or generic founder portrait.
2. One photographic subject, presented at its existing approved aspect ratio. Beside it, three short observational annotations: what the photograph shows, what hospitality experience asks, and what operations makes possible. The image stays recognizable; annotations change the reading rather than transform the photograph into decoration.
3. An editorial decision sheet connecting those perspectives to EXIF: observe what distinguishes the place, decide what deserves emphasis, direct how that value becomes visible.
4. A restrained rule and the heading “A personal letter / Una carta personal” lead into the existing founder letter. Preserve its complete wording, typography and disclosure behavior. New introductory copy must not paraphrase or substitute for the letter.

## Three meaningful moments

- **Choose a lens:** native buttons “Image / Experience / Operation” change short adjacent annotations about the same subject. Each view explains what it contributes and what it cannot establish alone. No image swaps, crop changes or implied evidence from an unresearched property. Keyboard and screen-reader selection are explicit.
- **See the decision:** one direct selection rearranges a typographic decision sheet from observations into hierarchy. A persistent explanation connects evidence, decision and consequence. This is a conceptual demonstration, not a client case.
- **Enter the letter:** a normal in-page link gives the reader control of the handoff to the original letter. No theatrical forced scrolling, pinning or concealed essential text.

On desktop, image and annotations form two columns with stable geometry. On mobile, the full subject precedes stacked annotations and buttons; no horizontally dragged narrative. Reduced motion changes states immediately. Without JavaScript, all perspectives and the complete letter remain readable. EN/ES variants receive equal visual priority and space.

## Risks and constraints

Photographic selection and rights need approval before implementation. Do not relocate, duplicate or crop protected gallery images to supply this concept. Use an approved subject with an explicit placement decision. Annotations must be authored as examples until property-specific research exists. The opening must not overstate founder experience or make the letter redundant. Limit the page to these three moments; keep navigation, routes, inquiry, analytics, type tokens and founder letter unchanged. Implementation requires separate approval.
